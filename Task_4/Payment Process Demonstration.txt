Here is the complete, step-by-step booking and payment walkthrough with all verified test credentials that bypass card restrictions.

---

### Step 1: Log In as a Patient

1. Open **`http://localhost:3000/login`** in your browser.
2. Sign in with standard test patient credentials (or your registered account):
   - **Email**: `patient1@medrella.test`
   - **Password**: `Password123!`

---

### Step 2: Select Doctor & Slot

1. Go to **`http://localhost:3000/doctors`**.
2. Click on any doctor (e.g. **Dr. Rajesh Sharma** — consultation fee: ₹800).
3. Select an available date and click an open time slot.
4. Click the blue button: **"Confirm Booking & Pay ₹800"**.
   - Medrella creates a **10-minute temporary seat hold** and initializes an official Razorpay order.

---

### Step 3: Launch Razorpay Checkout

In the popup modal that appears on your screen:
- Click **"Open Official Razorpay Checkout Popup"**.

---

### Step 4: Contact Details Screen

When Razorpay's popup asks for contact details:
- **Country**: Ensure the flag on the left is **India 🇮🇳 (+91)**.
- **Phone Number**: Enter:
  ```text
  9822012345
  ```
  *(or `9123456789` / your personal 10-digit mobile number)*
- **Email**: Enter:
  ```text
  patient@medrella.test
  ```
- Click **Proceed**.

---

### Step 5: Choose Payment Method & Enter Test Credentials

Choose **Option A** (Domestic Card) or **Option B** (UPI):

#### Option A: Domestic Card *(Avoids international card errors)*
- Click **Card**.
- Enter:
  | Field | Value |
  | :--- | :--- |
  | **Card Number** | `4718 6091 0820 4366` *(Visa Domestic)* <br> *or* `5267 3181 8797 5449` *(Mastercard Domestic)* |
  | **Expiry Date** | `12/28` *(any future MM/YY)* |
  | **CVV** | `123` |
  | **Card Holder** | `Patient` |
- Click **Pay ₹800**.
- On the bank authorization screen:
  - Enter OTP: **`1234`** *(or click the green **Success** button)*.

#### Option B: Test UPI *(Fastest method)*
- Click **UPI / QR**.
- In the UPI ID / VPA field, enter:
  ```text
  success@razorpay
  ```
- Click **Pay Now** (it completes instantly).

---

### Step 6: Confirmation & Receipt

1. Razorpay completes the transaction and returns the cryptographic signature.
2. Medrella verifies the signature on the server, commits the transaction, and locks your appointment with status **`BOOKED`**.
3. You will be directed to your dashboard where you can click **"Print Receipt"** or go to `http://localhost:3000/patient/payments` to see your printable invoice.

---

### Step 7: View on Razorpay Dashboard

1. Open **[https://dashboard.razorpay.com](https://dashboard.razorpay.com)**.
2. Make sure the toggle in the top bar is set to **Test Mode**.
3. Click **Transactions > Payments**:
   - You will see your payment for **₹800.00** marked as **Captured** with the customer details and matching Medrella Order ID.
4. Click **Transactions > Orders**:
   - You will see the corresponding order marked as **Paid**.
